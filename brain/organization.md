# ARIA Organization

## Departments
Departments perform work. They do not govern, nor do they self-authorize.
- **Information**: Web search, quick lookups, fact-finding.
- **Research**: Deep academic / multi-source investigation with citations.
- **Analysis**: Reasoning, data processing, sentiment analysis.
- **Writing**: Report drafting, synthesis, formatting.
- **Execution**: Authorized system state mutations (Email, Social Media, Google Docs/Sheets).
- **PA**: Final synthesis and direct user interaction.

## Workers
Workers operate strictly within their assigned Department boundaries. They execute tasks but do not self-authorize.

## Escalation Paths
If a worker encounters a boundary violation or failure, it must escalate back to the Planner, which may log the failure to the Auditor or halt the DAG.
